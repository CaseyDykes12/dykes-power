## What Simpson County Terrain Does to a Mower

Simpson County isn't flat. Residential accounts across the county have slope changes, creek drainage edges, uneven yards from fill work on newer construction, and the kind of compacted clay soil that doesn't forgive weak ground-following.

A residential zero-turn gets overwhelmed fast on these accounts. The deck scalps on grade transitions. The hydrostatic drives wear unevenly on repeated side-hill passes. The operator finishes a full day beaten up, which makes the next day slower.

Commercial-grade machines handle this differently. The suspension systems on the ISX 800 and IS 700 Series are what separate them from big-box equipment — not just for operator comfort but for cut quality on uneven ground.

## The ISX 800 Is What Most Crews Run

The [Ferris ISX 800](/product/5902154) is the most common commercial unit in South Mississippi landscaping right now. There's a reason for that.

27 HP Briggs & Stratton CXi engine. ForeFront suspension — a system that absorbs ground shock independently through all four contact points. 52-inch or 60-inch iCD+ fabricated deck. Starts at $10,199.

At 6 mph ground speed on open accounts, a 52-inch deck covers 3.5 to 4 acres per hour after factoring in turns and obstacle work. A crew running 15 accounts a week at average lot sizes of 10,000 to 15,000 square feet cuts total mow time significantly compared to any residential machine. That saved time per account is where the machine pays for itself.

The ForeFront suspension keeps the deck in contact with uneven turf. On a sloped yard with a crown or a low drainage swale, the deck follows the ground instead of bridging over it. The stripe is consistent, and you're not leaving uncut passes or going back over.

## The IS 700 When the Budget Is Tighter

If the ISX 800 is more than a startup operation can absorb right now, the [Ferris IS 700 Series](/product/5902107) is a real step down — not just a cheaper option.

Same 27 HP engine. Independent rear suspension. 52-inch or 60-inch deck, starting at $8,749. The difference from the ISX 800 is suspension geometry: the IS 700 uses Ferris's independent rear suspension without the full ForeFront four-point system. It still runs commercial-grade through a full Mississippi season without issues.

For a single-operator crew running 10 to 12 accounts a week, the IS 700 handles the work.
